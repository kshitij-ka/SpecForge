About SP‑21

India's Reference for Building Material Standards

BIS Special Publication 21 consolidates all Indian Standards relevant to building and construction materials — from Portland cement to wire ropes, sanitary fittings to structural steels. Published by the Bureau of Indian Standards, it is the authoritative handbook used by architects, structural engineers, contractors, and quality inspectors across the country.

This portal indexes the 2005 edition (929 pages) into 573 discrete IS standards with full-text search, keyword extraction, and section-level retrieval — making the handbook accessible without manual page-turning.
